# Contributing

Thanks for your interest. ENCORE. doesn't accept outside contributions to
this repository, so pull requests from outside Encore Music, Inc. will be
closed without review. Forks are welcome under the terms of the licence (see
`LICENSE` and `TRADEMARKS.md`).

To report a bug or a security problem, write to support@encoremusic.studio.
In the app, Help > Report a Problem makes a zip of the log and your audio setup
(no recordings) on your Desktop to send with it.
